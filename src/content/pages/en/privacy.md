title: Privacy
description: What this site collects when you subscribe, comment, or just read — and what it does not.
---

This site is static. Most pages are just files served to your browser with no
server-side processing and nothing written down about you. The exceptions are
listed below, in full, because that's the entire list.

## Email subscription

If you submit the subscribe form, your email address and chosen language are
sent to this site's server function, which:

- Signs a confirmation link containing your email address and sends it to you
  via [Resend](https://resend.com), the email delivery service. Resend acts
  as a processor for that message and, once you confirm, holds your address
  as a subscriber so future posts can be sent to you.
- Briefly stores your IP address in a counter (deleted after one hour) purely
  to stop the form from being spammed.
- Briefly stores the confirmation link itself (deleted once it expires or is
  used, whichever comes first — at most 24 hours) so the same link can't be
  used twice.

Every subscription email includes an unsubscribe option. Unsubscribing removes
you from Resend's list. To have your address removed entirely, or to ask what
is held about you, use the contact address on the [About](/about/) page.

## Comments

The comment section below each post is provided by
[Giscus](https://giscus.app), which stores comments as GitHub Discussions on
this site's repository, [ffzz/blog](https://github.com/ffzz/blog). **That
repository is public**, so your comment text and GitHub username are visible
to anyone and may be indexed by search engines. Commenting requires a GitHub
account and is governed
by [GitHub's privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) —
this site never sees your GitHub credentials.

## Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without
cookies, without fingerprinting, and without storing anything tied to you
individually. If you'd rather opt out of even that, most browsers' "block
third-party scripts" setting is effective, since it's loaded from a separate
domain.

## Everything else

Reading, searching, and browsing this site involves no accounts, no cookies
set by this site itself, and no tracking beyond what's described above.
